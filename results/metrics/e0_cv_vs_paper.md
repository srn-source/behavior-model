# E0 (5-fold CV) vs. the paper's LLM simulations, on all 2,058 people

Generated by `python -m scripts.run_e0_cv`. Do not edit by hand.

**What is directly comparable with the paper here:** the people (all 2,058), the LLM predictions (the dataset's own files), and the scoring routine (the paper's, reproduced to 3 decimals). The *Published* column is the number in the paper's summary file; *LLM (ours)* is the same file re-scored by our code and should match it.

**E0 here** is the population median fitted with 5-fold cross-validation (folds of 412, 412, 412, 411, 411 people, stratified on age × sex, seed 0): each person is predicted from the other folds only. E0 learns from other people's answers to these items (supervised-on-items); the LLMs do not (item-zero-shot). Δ is E0 − LLM, paired by person on the cells the LLM answered, with a 95% participant-bootstrap CI.

**Notes**

- *Published* is blank where the dataset ships no summary file (Demographics Only and the two Persona Summary specs) and for the wave-4 target, which the paper does not report.

- LLM Finetuning was trained on 500 participants; only the other 1,558 have predictions, and E0 is compared on exactly those people.
